Vrinda Cab Policies & Terms

1. Privacy & Passenger Data Protection

At Vrinda Cab, passenger confidentiality and security are paramount. We strictly collect only essential travel details (passenger name, phone number, pickup/drop coordinates, and travel date) solely to facilitate trip dispatch and driver coordination.

2. Zero Third-Party Sharing

Your personal contact numbers and trip itineraries are never sold, rented, or traded with third-party marketing brokers. Your telephone number is disclosed exclusively to your assigned commercial chauffeur 2 hours prior to pickup for vehicle arrival coordination.

3. Communications & Updates

We use WhatsApp and SMS solely for sending dispatch notifications, vehicle registration details, chauffeur verification badges, and formal GST tax invoices.

1. 0 Advance Booking Assurance

All outstation taxi rides with Vrinda Cab can be booked with 0 advance deposit. You inspect the vehicle and meet your verified chauffeur at your doorstep before embarking on the journey.

2. Kilometer & Duty Calculation

Outstation round-trips operate on a standardized transparent 250 km/calendar day basis. One-way drops are calculated based on point-to-point garage-to-drop routing or agreed flat locked package rates.

3. Night Driver Allowance

For journeys extending between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM, a nominal driver night allowance of ₹250 to ₹300 applies to support highway safety and chauffeur rest.

4. Commercial Fleet Standard

All vehicles in our network carry yellow commercial tourist transport plates, valid national interstate permits, comprehensive passenger insurance, and factory-certified speed governors (80 km/h).

1. 100% Free Cancellation

Plans change, and we understand. Passengers can cancel or reschedule any outstation cab booking completely free of charge anytime up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time.

2. No Locked Advances & Instant Relief

Because Vrinda Cab operates on a 0 Advance Booking model, passengers are never forced to wait 7 to 14 banking days for refund reconciliations. If you cancel, zero funds are held.

3. Flight / Train Delay Protection

For airport and railway transfers, we track live flight and train status. There is zero penalty or cancellation surcharge for delayed flights or trains.

1. Exact FASTag Deduction Transparency

All highway tolls on Yamuna Expressway, Eastern Peripheral, Delhi-Meerut Expressway, and Delhi-Mumbai Expressway are billed transparently as per actual government FASTag deductions. The driver displays the live SMS/receipt on request.

2. Interstate Border Commercial Permits

When entering neighboring states (Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Punjab), state entry passenger taxes are settled either at actual border checkposts or pre-included in our all-inclusive locked packages.

3. Parking & Airport Tolls

MCD commercial toll when entering Delhi and airport parking charges (if applicable at multi-level airport terminals) are borne directly at actuals.
